
# Definition

In the past, the state was called by the names of the 'Polis snd Cavitas' whose literal meaning is 'City State'. But, modern age is the period of 'Nation States'. Machiavelli, in this classic work 'The Prince', explaining the meaninf of State, states, "All the powers which had and have authority over men are states and are either monarchies or republics." According to Bluntschli, “The state is a politically organized people of definite territory."
