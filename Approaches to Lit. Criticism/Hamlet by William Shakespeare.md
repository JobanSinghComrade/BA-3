## The Story of Hamlet

## Introduction

_Hamlet_ is a very famous play. William Shakespeare wrote it more than 400 years ago. It is a sad story about a young prince, his family, and a terrible secret. This essay tells the whole story in simple English.

## The Sad Prince

The story is in Denmark. The king of Denmark is dead. His name is King Hamlet. His son is Prince Hamlet.

The prince is very sad. But there is more bad news. His uncle, Claudius, is now the new king. Claudius also married Queen Gertrude, Hamlet's mother. She married him only two months after the old king died. Hamlet thinks this is wrong. He is angry and unhappy.

## The Ghost

One night, some soldiers see a strange thing on the castle wall. It is a ghost! It looks like the dead king. The soldiers tell Horatio, Hamlet's best friend. Horatio tells Hamlet.

Hamlet goes to the wall at night. He sees the ghost too. The ghost speaks to him. It says:

"I am your father. Claudius did not let me die in a normal way. He killed me. He put poison in my ear while I was sleeping. You must take revenge! But please do not hurt your mother."

Hamlet is shocked. He promises to do it. But he is not sure if the ghost is telling the truth.

## Hamlet Pretends to Be Mad

Hamlet has a plan. He will act crazy. Then people will not watch him carefully, and he can learn the truth. He tells Horatio, but he tells no one else.

Now Hamlet says strange things and acts in strange ways. People begin to worry about him.

## Ophelia and Polonius

Polonius is the king's adviser. He has a son, Laertes, and a daughter, Ophelia. Ophelia loves Hamlet, and Hamlet loves her too. But Laertes and Polonius say, "Stay away from him. He is a prince. He will not marry you."

Ophelia listens to her family. She gives back Hamlet's letters and gifts. Hamlet is hurt. He speaks to her in an angry and confusing way. Polonius sees this. He thinks, "Hamlet is crazy because of love."

Claudius is not so sure. He asks two old school friends of Hamlet, Rosencrantz and Guildenstern, to spy on him. Hamlet quickly understands this. He does not trust them.

## The Play

Some actors come to the castle. Hamlet has an idea. He asks them to act a story about a king who is killed by poison. It is almost the same as his father's death.

Hamlet sits and watches King Claudius. During the play, Claudius becomes afraid. He stands up suddenly and leaves the room. Now Hamlet is sure. The ghost was telling the truth. Claudius is a murderer.

## The Prayer

Later, Hamlet finds Claudius alone. Claudius is on his knees and praying. Hamlet has a sword in his hand. It is the perfect moment! But Hamlet thinks, "If I kill him now, he will go to heaven. That is not right." So Hamlet waits. This is a big mistake.

## The Death of Polonius

Hamlet goes to his mother's room to talk. Polonius hides behind a curtain to listen. Hamlet hears a noise. He thinks it is Claudius. He pushes his sword through the curtain. But it is Polonius, and he is dead.

Then the ghost comes again. Only Hamlet can see it. Gertrude cannot see anything. She thinks her son is really mad.

## Hamlet Goes Away

Claudius is now afraid of Hamlet. He sends him to England with Rosencrantz and Guildenstern. He also gives them a secret letter. The letter says, "Kill Hamlet when he arrives."

But on the ship, Hamlet finds the letter. He changes it. Now it says, "Kill Rosencrantz and Guildenstern." Then pirates attack the ship. In the fight, Hamlet jumps onto the pirate ship. The pirates help him return to Denmark. His two old friends go to England and are killed.

## Ophelia's Sad End

While Hamlet is away, Ophelia has a terrible time. Her father is dead, and she loses her mind from sadness. She walks around the castle and sings strange songs. One day, she goes to a river. She falls into the water and dies.

Laertes comes back from France. He is very angry about his father's death and his sister's death. He wants revenge on Hamlet.

## The Plan Against Hamlet

Claudius sees a chance. He talks to Laertes and makes a plan:

1. Laertes and Hamlet will have a friendly sword fight.
2. Laertes will use a sword with poison on the point.
3. If that does not work, there will be a cup of poisoned wine for Hamlet.

Laertes agrees.

## The Graveyard

Hamlet comes home with Horatio. In a graveyard, they see two men digging a grave. One of them finds a skull. It belongs to Yorick, the king's old joker. Hamlet knew him when he was a child. He holds the skull and thinks about life and death. "Everyone dies," he says, "kings and poor people, the same."

Then a funeral arrives. It is Ophelia's funeral. Hamlet learns that she is dead. He is heartbroken. Laertes jumps into the grave and Hamlet joins him. They fight. Hamlet says, "I loved Ophelia more than forty thousand brothers could love her."

## The Final Fight

Now it is time for the sword fight. Everyone in the court comes to watch. Claudius puts a cup of wine on the table.

At first, Hamlet is winning. Gertrude is happy. She picks up the cup and drinks the wine to celebrate. Claudius tries to stop her, but it is too late. The wine has poison in it!

Then Laertes cuts Hamlet with the poisoned sword. In the fight, the swords fall and change hands. Hamlet cuts Laertes with the same poisoned sword.

Suddenly, Gertrude falls down. "The wine! The wine! I am poisoned!" she cries, and she dies.

Laertes is dying too. He says, "Hamlet, it is the king's plan. The sword is poisoned. The wine is poisoned. The king is to blame." He asks Hamlet to forgive him. Hamlet does, and Laertes dies.

Now Hamlet is angry. He takes the sword and stabs Claudius. Then he makes Claudius drink the poisoned wine. Claudius dies.

## The End

Hamlet is also dying from the poison. He says to Horatio, "Please tell my story to everyone. Tell them the truth." Horatio wants to die with his friend, but Hamlet asks him to live. Then Hamlet dies.

At that moment, Fortinbras, a prince from Norway, arrives. He sees all the dead people. Horatio tells him everything. Fortinbras becomes the new ruler of Denmark. He orders that Hamlet must have a funeral like a brave soldier.

## What Can We Learn?

_Hamlet_ is a play about many big ideas:

- **Revenge:** Wanting revenge can destroy many lives, not just one.
- **Thinking too long:** Hamlet thinks and thinks, and he waits too long to act. Many people die because of this.
- **Truth and lies:** In Denmark, many people lie, spy, and pretend. The truth is hard to find.
- **Life and death:** Hamlet often asks, "What is life? What happens when we die?"

That is why people still love this story today.
